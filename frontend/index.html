S.E.T.U Architecture & Methodology

1. Why Hybrid TransUNet?

Traditional CNNs excel at local pixel features but fail to understand "global context," leading to fragmented roads under clouds or shadows.

Our Hybrid TransUNet fixes this: The CNN Encoder captures the local road textures, and the Transformer (Self-Attention) mechanism captures the global trajectory. The AI learns to "hallucinate" logical road continuity beneath occlusions.

2. Topological Healing (Union-Find & MST)

To satisfy ISRO requirements, we utilize Disjoint Sets (Union-Find) and Minimum Spanning Trees (MST) to mathematically bridge missing links.

Technical Risk Mitigation: How do we prevent the algorithm from connecting two parallel highways 20 meters apart? We apply strict heuristic constraints:
1. Euclidean distance < 60px.
2. Angular Alignment (Cosine Similarity) > 0.25 (Rejects impossible 90° connections).
